About this design ↓

Each room has its own full-screen photograph, kept crisp with no blur. Translucent off-white panels keep the menu readable while the room remains visible around them. Room-preview controls are for this design study only; a guest menu would stay tied to its room-specific QR link.

Victorian scrollwork, double-rule frames and numbered medallions give the menu more of the house’s character. The real Bissing House logo remains static so guests reach their choices quickly.

Full menus stay together, with guest shortcuts to reduce scrolling. Egg preparation appears only for Eggs; toppings appear for Pancake, Crepe and French Toast. Checks and outlines show selections without depending on color.

The review lets guests edit either meal or time without losing their choices. Missing mains, egg preparation or dining time receive specific messages. Sides, drinks and toppings remain optional in this concept.

Continental is a proposed separate path: it bypasses cooked-meal requirements and lists each guest as Continental. Its contents and service details are intentionally unspecified.

Colors use exact values from the website styles: plum #4C3146, pale blue #DEEFEF, blue-teal #1D4354, cream #EFEFDE, off-white #FAF8FA, charcoal #3F3F3F and white #FFFFFF. The original logo retains its own image colors.

Concept only: no database, messages, real orders or saved guest information. Logo and styling assets load from external hosts; breakfast choices never leave this page.
